# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to **5e Character Creator & Sheet**, developed by Rammy Canales.

## Data Collection

**5e Character Creator & Sheet** does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information.

Character information and other data created within the app are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to the developer or to an external server.

## Accounts

**5e Character Creator & Sheet** does not require users to create an account or sign in.

## Purchases

The app may offer in-app purchases through Google Play.

Payment processing is handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store users' payment card information.

Google may process information related to purchases in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

## Analytics and Advertising

**5e Character Creator & Sheet** does not use advertising services.

**5e Character Creator & Sheet** does not use third-party analytics services to track users.

## Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because the developer does not collect or store personal user data, there is no personal data retained on the developer's servers.

Data stored locally by the app can be removed by deleting the relevant information within the app or uninstalling the app.

## Children's Privacy

**5e Character Creator & Sheet** is not specifically directed toward children.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's data practices or features change. The latest version will always be available at this page.
